About

I care most about accuracy in distributed systems, databases, and concurrency. Tiny details decide whether a system stays correct or quietly falls apart.

Currently, I am an intern at Clockwork Labs, where I am building SpacetimeDB, a distributed real-time database that combines the database, application server, and synchronization layers into one high-performance environment.

I have also worked on scheduling performance and security at Cal.com and on GPU attestation and deterministic evidence pipelines at Ashiba Research. I have the most respect for code with clear, unambiguous, and explicit invariants.

Right now, I am drawn to real-time infrastructure, distributed databases, backend performance, open source systems software, indexing, search, schema design, correctness, and technical writing that makes complex systems easier to understand.
